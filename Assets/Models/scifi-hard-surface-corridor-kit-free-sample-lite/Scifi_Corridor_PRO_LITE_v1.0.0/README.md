# SciFi Corridor PRO LITE v1.0.0

A free sample of SciFi Modular Corridor PRO: 6 pieces copied unchanged from the PRO v1.0.0 release.

- Corridor_Straight
- Corridor_End
- Door_Slide_Closed
- Crate_Small
- Locker_Single
- Console_Desk

Every piece: GLB + FBX in `models/` and `meta/<Name>.json`. The three props (crate, locker, console) also have LOD1/LOD2 GLBs in `lods/` and a collision GLB in `collision/`, as in PRO. The 29 textures they use are in `textures/`. Units are metres, Y up in GLB; the corridor pieces sit on the PRO kit's 4 m grid.

The full kit has 69 pieces, a worn variant of each, and two demo scenes: https://hopefulsham.itch.io/scifi-modular-corridor-pro
