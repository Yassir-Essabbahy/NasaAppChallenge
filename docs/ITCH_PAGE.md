## Description

**The sun is far away.**
You are **Commander Alex Vale**, recovering inside **Outpost Helios** with three crewmates—**Dr. Maya Lin** (Medic), **Samir Patel** (Engineer), and **Dr. Elena Rostova** (Scientist). Rescue arrives on **Sol 7**. Between now and then, every watt of solar electricity, drop of recycled water, and hour of radiation shielding counts.

### What You'll Do
- **Walk the Outpost in First Person:** Inspect the Command Module, Medbay, Greenhouse, Storage Bay, and Airlock in chunky PSX-inspired 3D.
- **Suit Up & Step Onto Mars:** Put on your EVA suit at the rack (`E` or `T`), cycle the airlock safely, and head onto the Martian surface past solar arrays, hazard beacons, and a Perseverance-style rover.
- **Command `OUTPOST OS` (or Classic View):** Balance solar power across Life Support, Greenhouse, Heating, and Earth Comms, spend 3 daily Action Points, read delayed radio messages from Earth, and preview tonight's forecast before ending each sol.
- **Learn Real Space Science:** Every crisis features a plain-language **NASA Science Fact** card, and the in-terminal **Sources** app clearly labels every constant as `REAL`, `ADAPTED`, or `GAME` with links to NASA, ESA, and USGS research.
- **Play in English or Arabic:** Choose your language right at startup with the bilingual language slider. Includes pause-menu look sensitivity, audio/VOX volume, larger text, flicker reduction, and PSX visual toggles.

## Controls

| Key / Input | Action |
|---|---|
| `W` `A` `S` `D` | Move |
| `Left Shift` | Sprint |
| `Space` | Jump *(also skips intro/end-sol cards)* |
| `Left Control` | Crouch |
| `Mouse` | Look around *(adjust sensitivity in Pause Menu `Esc`)* |
| `Right Click` | Camera zoom |
| `E` | Interact (Terminal, Suit Rack, Doors, Airlock, Station Inspection) / End Sol inside Terminal |
| `T` | Quick toggle EVA Suit on/off *(inside pressurized habitat only)* |
| `1` – `9` | Switch apps inside Terminal |
| `Enter` | Confirm modal or continue screen |
| `Esc` | Leave Terminal / Open Pause Menu |
| `Left` / `Right Arrow` | Switch English / Arabic on startup language slider |
