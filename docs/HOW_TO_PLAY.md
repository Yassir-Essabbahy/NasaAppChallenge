# How to Play — Mars Outpost: Last Shift

*A quick guide for students, junior astronauts, and teachers.*

---

## 1. Your Goal

Keep **Commander Alex Vale** and the 4-person crew of **Ares-4** alive for **7 Martian days (sols)** until the rescue craft lands on **Sol 7**.
Every sol, you must balance electricity, breathable air, drinking water, food, radiation shielding, and crew health/morale.

---

## 2. Exploring the Base & Opening the Terminal

1. Look around with the **Mouse** and walk with **`W A S D`** (**`Left Shift`** to sprint, **`Space`** to jump, **`Left Control`** to crouch).
2. Walk up to the glowing monitor in the **Command Module** and press **`E`** to open **OUTPOST OS**.
3. You can use two terminal views at any time using the mode toggle button in the top bar:
   - **Desktop OS:** Retro-futuristic desktop with draggable app windows, a taskbar, widgets, and status chips.
   - **Classic View:** Full-screen dashboard with a fixed left sidebar.
   - Press **`1`–`9`** to jump between apps quickly, or press **`Esc`** to leave the terminal and walk around the outpost again.

---

## 3. Balancing Power Allocation

Open **Power Grid** (`Life Support`, `Greenhouse`, `Heating`, and `Comms` sliders):

- Solar panels generate electricity during the day (`60` max base output), reduced by **Panel Dust** and **Dust Storms**.
- **Keep your Battery above 0%!** If the battery hits `0%` overnight, a random machine (`CO2 Scrubber`, `Water Recycler`, or `Greenhouse`) can break down.
- **Keep Heating at 50% or higher!** Dropping heating below `50%` makes the habitat freeze, injuring the crew and hurting morale.
- **Comms (`Earth Radio`)** lets delayed messages arrive from Earth. You can turn it down during an emergency, or use the **Safe / Balanced / Save Power** preset buttons to test combinations and preview tonight's **Forecast** before ending the sol.

---

## 4. Action Points & Daily Duties

Each sol gives your crew **3 Action Points (`AP`)** for hands-on jobs:

- **Clean Solar Panels (EVA):** `1 AP` — Removes `40%` dust, adds `+0.55 mSv` EVA radiation dose (reduced by shielding).
- **Repair Damaged System:** `1 AP` — Fixes the most damaged life-support or crop machine (`Engineer` Samir Patel adds a `+15%` repair bonus).
- **Reinforce Shielding:** `1 AP`, costs `8` Power — Adds `+14%` radiation shielding.
- **Rest & Hot Meal:** `1 AP` — Boosts all living crew `+15` Morale and `+4` HP.
- **Treat Crew Member:** `1 AP` — Heals the most injured crew member (`Medic` Dr. Maya Lin adds `+10` HP).
- **Ration Supplies:** `1 AP` — Cuts food and water usage by `25%` this sol at a `-8` Morale cost.

### Daily Shift Checklist (`0/3` -> `3/3`)

Before clicking **End Sol** (`E` inside the terminal), complete all **3 daily tasks** shown in your duty tracker:
1. **Resolve Today's Event** (choose how the crew responds to the daily alert).
2. **Run a Command Action** (tune power on Sol 1, or spend at least `1 AP` on later sols).
3. **Complete Your Physical Inspection Round:** Leave the terminal (`Esc`) and walk to the assigned station shown on your HUD (such as Medbay to check Cmdr. Vale, Storage to inspect the O2/Water tanks, the Greenhouse planters, Desk Radio, or Surface stations outside), then press **`E`**. *(During Dust Storms or Solar Flares, outdoor inspections switch safely to an indoor backup station.)*

---

## 5. Events & NASA Science Cards

Every sol brings an outpost event—such as **Solar Panel Dust Build-up**, **CO2 Scrubber Clog**, **Martian Dust Storm**, **Solar Particle Event**, or **Message from Earth** (with realistic 4–24 minute one-way radio light delay).
Read the options carefully: some spend `AP`, power, or supplies to prevent bigger damage later. Every event also includes a **NASA Science Fact** explaining the real space science behind it!

---

## 6. EVA Suit & Airlock Rules

When your shift requires walking outside onto the Martian surface:
1. Go to the **Suit Rack** in the Command Module and press **`E`** (or press **`T`** anywhere inside the pressurized base) to **Put on Suit**.
2. Walk to the **Airlock** and press **`E`** to cycle through and step outside onto Mars.
3. **Safety Rules:**
   - The airlock will **refuse to cycle outward** unless you are wearing your EVA suit.
   - You **cannot take off your suit** while outside on Mars or while the airlock is cycling/depressurized.

---

## 7. Winning, Grades & Losing

- **Victory:** Keep at least one crew member alive through **Sol 7** until rescue arrives. Your mission grade (`S`, `A`, `B`, or `C`) scores surviving crew (`50%`), average crew HP (`30%`), and remaining supplies (`20%`). All `4` crew surviving with a score `>= 88` earns an **S**!
- **Mission Lost (`F`):** If all `4` crew members reach `0` HP, the outpost fails.
- After any mission ends, open **"What NASA Knows"** on the debrief screen (or open the **Sources** app anytime) to explore the real space research behind the game.

---

## 8. Quick Tips for Junior Commanders

- On **Sol 1**, check **Dr. Maya Lin's** treat action—Commander Alex Vale starts injured at `60` HP!
- **Clean solar panels early** before a dust storm traps you indoors with low sunlight.
- Keep **CO2 Scrubber** and **Water Recycler** health high so you don't lose extra oxygen or crew HP.
- Don't panic if stocks dip slightly during a storm—just keep **Battery > 0%** and **Heating >= 50%**, and watch the **Forecast** numbers before clicking **End Sol**.

---

## 9. Classroom Discussion Questions (For Teachers & Students)

1. **Trade-offs in a Dust Storm:** During a Martian dust storm when solar power drops sharply, which systems would you turn down first (`Greenhouse`, `Comms`, `Heating`, or `Life Support`), and why? What happened to NASA's solar-powered *Opportunity* rover in 2018?
2. **Closed-Loop Living:** Why can't astronauts on Mars call for a quick delivery truck full of water and oxygen? How do ISS systems like the 98% water recycler and Perseverance's MOXIE experiment solve that problem?
3. **Invisible Danger:** Unlike Earth, Mars has no global magnetic field and only a thin atmosphere. How does radiation dose (`mSv`) change the choices you make about going outside (`EVA`) versus staying behind shielded habitat walls?
4. **Living Together Far From Home:** Why does growing fresh plants in a greenhouse affect both food supply *and* crew morale—and what does NASA's year-long CHAPEA study teach us about teamwork in small habitats?
5. **No Live Video Calls:** If a radio signal takes 4 to 24 minutes to go one way between Earth and Mars, how would you design mission rules so a crew doesn't have to wait for Earth's permission during an emergency?
